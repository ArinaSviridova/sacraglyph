This Privacy Policy explains what data may be collected through the bazookatattoo website, why it is used and how it is handled.

This website is used for communication with tattoo artist Nikita Presnyakov / bazookatattoo.

  • name submitted in the contact form;

3. Why data is used

5. User rights

You can request correction or deletion of your data by contacting the channels listed on the website.
