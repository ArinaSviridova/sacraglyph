Tattoo in Tbilisi

Nikita «Bazooka» Tattoo

I approach tattooing as a form of visual writing. The main directions of my work are lettering, calligraphy and abstract graphics. What interests me in them is the gesture of the line, the rhythm of the sign and the energy between emptiness and form.

Sacraglyph is the name of my visual language. Through it I explore symbol, ornament and the structure of writing, turning them into compositions that live on the body as naturally as on paper or canvas.

For me, what matters is not only the tattoo itself, but how it interacts with the body. I work with line direction, scale and the plasticity of form so the tattoo looks cohesive and keeps its expressiveness over time.

Each piece is created individually. I pay close attention to the idea and search for the exact composition and form that honestly reflects the person.

If this aesthetic resonates with you, message me and we can discuss your future piece.

Lettering and calligraphy

Text, sign, rhythm, form and line gesture. I create lettering and calligraphic compositions without templates.

Blackwork and graphic style

Dense forms, contrast, abstract graphics and compositions that stay cohesive and expressive on the body.

Freehand

Some projects are built directly around the anatomy of the body. This helps find a more natural and precise composition.

How booking works

A quick overview of how a tattoo project starts.

1. Idea

You message me through the form, Telegram, WhatsApp or Instagram and describe what you want to do.

2. Discussion

We discuss the size, placement, style, timing and estimate. Consultation can happen in person or remotely. References and project details can be discussed as needed.

3. Session

On the session day, I prepare the composition and do the tattoo in a comfortable studio setting, with breaks when needed. Numbing is also possible.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about booking and pricing

How much does a tattoo cost?

The first hour costs 300 GEL, each following hour costs 250 GEL. Full day sessions cost 1200 GEL. The final time and price depend on placement, size and complexity. Exact details are discussed before booking.

Can I book in Batumi?

Yes. Sessions are available in Tbilisi and Batumi. Please confirm the current city and date before booking.

Do you work freehand?

Yes, some projects are created freehand when it works better for anatomy and composition.
